# Food and beverage decisions

Choose the page contract through `references/page-types/_index.md`. This
reference retains vertical-specific decision inputs, not section ordering,
static island recipes, offer policy, or another visual system.

## Product decisions

### Product facts

Make serving size, pack quantity, ingredient list, allergens and preparation requirements easy to find. A dietary label needs the actual product evidence.

### Consumption context

Distinguish taste, texture, preparation, portability and meal occasion. The angle determines which real product job the image must demonstrate.

### Image decisions

Prioritize packaging identity, serving scale, preparation sequence and finished serving. A generic ingredient scene does not replace the actual product.

### Replenishment

Base the cadence on real pack size and the merchant selling plans. Keep one-time and subscription commitments distinguishable at the decision point.

### Bundles

Explain included items, flavors, quantities and the shopper reason for the combination. Availability and price come from the real kit or its components.

### Purchase concerns

Show storage, shelf-life, dispatch, delivery geography and temperature-handling terms when relevant and verified.
