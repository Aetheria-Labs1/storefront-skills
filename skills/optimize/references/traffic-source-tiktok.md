# TikTok traffic

This reference holds traffic-specific decisions. `references/page-types/_index.md`
owns anatomy; `references/copy/message-match.md` owns message-match assessment.

## Continue the creator context

Capture the creator angle, demonstrated product, spoken promise and next action. Keep paid/creator disclosures with the relevant content and preserve the relationship to the clicked clip.

## Choose the video role

A short demonstration, a creator story and a long sales video serve different jobs. Let the chosen page type and `references/assets/video-rules.md` decide placement, controls and playback.

## Preserve useful specificity

Retain the concrete demonstration or shopper question that earned the click. Do not imitate platform slang or invent a customer voice because the traffic source is TikTok.

## Design for the entry context

Review the hosted page on mobile with readable purchase context, captions and user-controlled media. Native feel is not permission to reduce accessibility or hide terms.

## Evaluate continuity

Compare the first visible product, claim and action with the clip and measure the intended funnel event. A visually similar page still fails if it changes the product or promise.

## Execution

Use `references/ad-to-page.md` for creative extraction and
`references/workflows/_how-to-read.md` for the page workflow. Proof, offers,
assets and house styling retain their own authorities. This file supplies
context for those decisions, not a second set of rendering rules.
