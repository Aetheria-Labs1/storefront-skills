# Fashion decisions

Choose the page contract through `references/page-types/_index.md`. This
reference retains vertical-specific decision inputs, not section ordering,
static island recipes, offer policy, or another visual system.

## Product decisions

### Fit

Treat sizing, body measurements, garment measurements and intended fit as distinct information. Place the relevant size guidance where the variant choice happens.

### Variants

Coordinate color names, swatches and per-variant photography from the actual catalogue. The selected size/color and availability must agree with the purchase control.

### Material

Use fabric, weave, weight, stretch, transparency and care information when supplied. Describe the observed construction rather than generic luxury adjectives.

### Image decisions

Prioritize front/back, detail, scale and fit jobs. A campaign crop and a product inspection image serve different purposes; the page type decides where each belongs.

### Collections

A lookbook groups a coherent outfit or scene; a collection groups discoverable products. Keep product links and availability clear when moving between those experiences.

### Purchase concerns

Explain shipping, returns and exchanges using the actual policy. Do not promise a fit guarantee or universally free returns without evidence.
