# Beauty decisions

Choose the page contract through `references/page-types/_index.md`. This
reference retains vertical-specific decision inputs, not section ordering,
static island recipes, offer policy, or another visual system.

## Product decisions

### Suitability

Identify the skin/hair concern, routine position, texture preference and exclusions before choosing the angle. Do not present the same product as suitable for everyone.

### Variants

A shade, finish or size is a purchase decision. Preserve the exact catalogue variant and its media; keep shade names legible rather than relying on color alone.

### Product explanation

Show packaging, texture, application amount and how the product fits the routine. Ingredients and mechanisms need the merchant evidence appropriate to the actual claim.

### Image decisions

Prioritize texture, scale, shade/finish and application jobs. An identity image is not evidence of an in-use result. Use the vertical imagery owner for source eligibility.

### Proof context

Keep routine, skin/hair context and stated limitations with a review when the source carries them. Before/after media is a separate evidence decision, not an aesthetic treatment.

### Purchase concerns

Clarify quantity, expected use, shipping and returns from the merchant context. Complementary routine products must solve a stated relationship, not fill a generic recommendation row.
