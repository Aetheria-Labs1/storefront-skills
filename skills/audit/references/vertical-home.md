# Home and living decisions

Choose the page contract through `references/page-types/_index.md`. This
reference retains vertical-specific decision inputs, not section ordering,
static island recipes, offer policy, or another visual system.

## Product decisions

### Dimensions

Give width, height, depth and units where supplied; distinguish product, packaged and usable dimensions. Placement decisions need scale, not a decorative room image alone.

### Material

Describe construction, finish, texture and care using product facts. A detail image and a room scene answer different questions.

### Variants

Preserve finish, size and configuration relationships. Compare the selected variant with the room/scale imagery rather than assuming every photograph shows the same configuration.

### Image decisions

Prioritize scale, dimensions, material detail, included items and placement. Use the imagery owner for exact-product and source requirements.

### Purchase concerns

Clarify assembly, access, delivery method, installation and returns when the merchant supplies them. Do not substitute a generic shipping badge for large-item delivery terms.

### Merchandising

Related products should complete a verified use case or room requirement. Keep accessories distinguishable from what is included in the price.
