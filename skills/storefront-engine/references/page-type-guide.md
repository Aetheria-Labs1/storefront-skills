# Page Type Guide

Choose exactly one type before templates. Use traffic, awareness, product
count, desired action, purchase model, and offer. The section sequence is a
default; record every omission or deviation in the plan.

## Decision Route

1. If the goal is not purchase, choose the closest non-purchase type:
   quiz, lead capture, waitlist, story, science, browse, support, loyalty,
   post-purchase, or wholesale.
2. If the goal is purchase and traffic is cold, choose by entry promise:
   ad landing, advertorial, listicle, creator, video sales, comparison, buyer's
   guide, trial, or search-intent PDP.
3. If the visitor is warm or hot, choose by purchase model:
   PDP, hybrid PDP, bundle, subscription, offer, sale, gifting, restock, or
   retargeting.

## Types and Default Anatomy

| Type | Use when | Default section sequence |
|---|---|---|
| `ad-landing-page` | cold paid traffic, one product, one action | message-matched hero, trust facts, problem, mechanism, proof, benefits, buy box, FAQ, closing CTA |
| `pdp` | full product detail inside store navigation | gallery + buy box, key benefits, details/materials, use/fit, proof, shipping/returns, FAQ, recommendations |
| `pdp-hybrid-landing` | paid traffic needs PDP buying depth without navigation | message band, gallery + buy box, mechanism, benefits, proof, comparison, FAQ, closing CTA |
| `advertorial` | cold traffic needs a long editorial argument | disclosure/dateline, editorial hero, hook, failed alternatives, mechanism, proof, product bridge, FAQ |
| `listicle` | numbered reasons frame drives the click | numbered hero, reason sections, proof, comparison, offer bridge, FAQ, closing CTA |
| `comparison-us-vs-them` | visitor evaluates named alternatives | switching hero, criteria/method, comparison, evidence, concession, product offer, FAQ |
| `quiz-funnel` | visitor needs diagnosis or routing | promise, qualification, quiz, result logic, recommendation, capture/CTA, privacy |
| `bundle-kit` | products solve one job together | bundle hero, included items, routine/assembly, savings, builder or selection, proof, FAQ |
| `offer-page` | a specific discount, gift, or code is primary | offer hero, eligibility/terms, product, value, proof, urgency only if dated, FAQ, CTA |
| `sale-clearance-flash` | multiple discounted products share a real window | sale hero, terms/end, filtered products, shipping, proof, FAQ |
| `seasonal-gifting` | occasion, recipient, or delivery cutoff drives choice | occasion hero, recipient/price routes, products, gifting services, delivery, proof, FAQ |
| `gift-guide` | browse gifts without a hard campaign window | guide hero, recipient/price groups, recommendations, delivery/gift options, FAQ |
| `launch-waitlist-preorder` | product is upcoming or pre-orderable | launch hero, problem/vision, product reveal, founder/proof, roadmap, terms, capture or pre-order |
| `restock` | known product is newly available again | restock hero, product + buy box, what returned, proof, availability facts, FAQ |
| `subscription` | recurring purchase is central | subscription hero, plan options, savings, cadence, flexibility/cancellation, proof, FAQ |
| `ugc-creator-collab` | rights-cleared creator content is the entry point | creator hero, product spotlight, creator proof/story, benefits, offer, FAQ |
| `video-sales-page` | one video carries the main argument | video headline, player, transcript/summary, benefits, proof, offer, FAQ |
| `brand-story-founder` | trust in the brand story is the goal | story hero, origin, values, founder/team, process, proof, products, CTA |
| `ingredient-science` | mechanism, ingredients, materials, or studies decide | science hero, mechanism, ingredient/material sections, evidence, safety/use, product, FAQ |
| `collection-landing` | visitor browses one category | collection hero, category routes/filters, product grid, guidance, proof, FAQ |
| `homepage` | whole-store orientation | brand/value hero, category routes, featured products, differentiation, proof, story, capture, footer |
| `lookbook-shop-the-look` | visual combinations drive product discovery | lookbook hero, shoppable looks, item details, styling guidance, recommendations |
| `thank-you-post-purchase` | visitor has just completed purchase | confirmation, next steps, usage/onboarding, support, referral or relevant cross-sell |
| `lead-capture-giveaway` | email/phone capture is the conversion | incentive hero, value/terms, form, privacy/consent, proof, FAQ |
| `referral-loyalty-vip` | programme participation is the goal | programme hero, how it works, tiers/rewards, eligibility, proof, join CTA, FAQ |
| `retargeting-warm` | prior view/cart action and one objection are known | return hero, product + buy box, primary objection, proof, offer if real, FAQ |
| `faq-support-led` | questions and policy confidence are primary | support hero/search, topic groups, detailed answers, contact/escalation, policy links |
| `trial-sample` | low-risk trial precedes full purchase | trial hero, what is included, eligibility/cost, how it works, proof, conversion path, FAQ |
| `wholesale-b2b` | reseller or bulk buyer is the visitor | wholesale hero, fit/requirements, catalogue/MOQ, margins/terms, proof, application |
| `seo-buyers-guide` | search visitor wants ranked buying guidance | answer-first hero, methodology, criteria, recommendations, comparison, FAQ, sources |

## Page-Type Check

Before approval confirm:

- the type matches the desired action and awareness;
- the first screen answers the entry question;
- purchase controls appear only when the visitor is ready for them;
- proof sits beside the claim it supports;
- every required policy or offer term is visible before commitment;
- each section has a distinct decision job;
- desktop and mobile preserve the same narrative;
- omitted default sections and custom additions have reasons.
