# Reference PDP adaptation

Capture a supplied product-page reference to understand its purchase flow,
media jobs and information hierarchy. The current merchant's product and
policy evidence remain authoritative.

## Capture and decompose

Record URL, viewport, product/variant, gallery behavior, purchase context,
shipping/return placement and optional interactions. Keep screenshots and
notes tied to the actual page version. Do not infer hidden behavior from a
single image.

Map observations to `references/page-types/pdp.md` or
`references/page-types/pdp-hybrid-landing.md`. Keep unique useful choices,
such as material inspection or compatibility explanation, as explicit
section decisions. There is no fixed reference-to-island lookup table.

## Adapt and build

Read current catalogue and merchant evidence, execute
`references/workflows/_how-to-read.md`, and resolve interactive behavior
through live schemas. Use the merchant's source-eligible media instead of
copying reference product shots or proof. Source, compile, draft and edit
transitions follow `references/generation-protocol.md`.

## Compare the result

Review the hosted draft at 390px and 1280px against the intended hierarchy,
media emphasis, purchase clarity and mobile reading order. Test required
interactions, especially variant and selling-plan changes, rather than
assuming a visual match proves behavior. Report intentional differences,
missing evidence and untested states. No automatic publication follows.
