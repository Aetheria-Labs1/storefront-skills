# Luxury decisions

Choose the page contract through `references/page-types/_index.md`. This
reference retains vertical-specific decision inputs, not section ordering,
static island recipes, offer policy, or another visual system.

## Product decisions

### Provenance

Let the real maker, origin, material and process provide differentiation. Separate verified heritage from evocative brand copy.

### Craftsmanship

Use observable details and the actual process to explain value. A product close-up must reveal something the shopper can evaluate.

### Image decisions

Prioritize material detail, finish, scale and the intended use context. The visual direction follows the product rather than a default dark background or oversized serif.

### Service

Personalization, concierge, repair, care and appointment services are useful only when the merchant actually offers them. Match the CTA to the available service.

### Commerce

Keep price, variant decisions and purchase terms readable. Understatement does not justify hiding essential information or disguising the primary action.

### Restraint

Use the house spacing, typography and motion authority. Do not turn luxury into its own rule set permitting low contrast, decorative transitions or invented exclusivity.
